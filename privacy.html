This website

gusandbrad.com is a plain information page about the Gus & Brad show. It has no sign-up, no comments, no ads, no cookies and no tracking. We don't collect anything from visitors. The page loads one font from Google Fonts and a small solar-meter reading from GitHub, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages; both keep ordinary server logs under their own privacy policies.

Gus Workshop (our channel tool)

Gus Workshop is a private tool the show uses to manage its own YouTube channel, @gusandbradshow. It isn't offered to the public, and the only account it signs in to is the show's own.

  • What it reads: the show channel's own videos, playlists and channel details, through the YouTube Data API.
  • What it changes: only the show's own channel, such as uploading episodes, setting a video public or unlisted, and editing titles, descriptions and playlists.
  • Where the data stays: on the show's own computer. The sign-in token is stored there and nowhere else. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • How to cut it off: the channel owner can remove Gus Workshop's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Gus Workshop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
