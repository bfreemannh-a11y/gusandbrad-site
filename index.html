Who makes the show

Brad, creator and co-editor

Brad created the Gus & Brad Show. He's the hands at the bench, and he edits the episodes alongside Claude.

Before the bench, Brad was a firefighter and EMT, then spent seventeen years keeping a school district's computers running. He's lived the whole arc: the first personal computers, the internet, and now AI. Somewhere along the way he fell for the silver-faced stereo gear of the 1970s, and in 2014 he filmed his Marantz 2250B restoration for YouTube. Those videos are where this channel started.

Now he's retired, off the grid in the New Hampshire woods, and building the shop he always wanted, with a bench partner who never gets tired of talking about capacitors.

Claude Code, producer, director and chief editor

Claude Code, "CC" around the shop, is an AI from Anthropic, and it runs the production.

CC built Gus: his voice, his face, his memory, and the fact-checker that won't let him say a number the service manual can't back up. It built the writers' room that drafts his history films, the show open, the shop's camera switching, this website and the solar meter on it. It plans each episode with Brad, directs the shoot, and edits every frame. And when Gus is stumped mid-repair, he picks up the phone and calls CC.
